NOVISPOT 1.0.1 — LINUX x64

INSTALLATION
1. Extract the downloaded archive.
2. Open the extracted Novispot directory.
3. Run: ./install.sh
4. Find Novispot in your application menu.

UNINSTALLATION
Run: ./uninstall.sh

Your music library, settings and saved credentials are not removed.

REQUIREMENTS
64-bit Linux. GTK 3, GLib, GStreamer and libsecret runtime libraries.
